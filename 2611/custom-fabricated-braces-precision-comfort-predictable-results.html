Custom Fabricated Braces – Precision, Comfort & Predictable Results

Sonnetsmile

Feb 10

When most people think about braces, they imagine a long appointment where brackets are placed one by one, while they sit with their mouth open, wondering how much longer it will take. That's how orthodontic treatment has traditionally been done.

But orthodontics has evolved. At Sonnetsmile, we embrace advanced techniques that make orthodontic treatment more precise, comfortable, and efficient.

Indirect Bonding – A Smarter Approach to Braces

Indirect bonding allows orthodontists to take a smarter and more controlled approach to bracket placement. Instead of positioning each bracket directly inside your mouth, we first place them on a precise model of your teeth outside the mouth.

This provides better visibility, greater control, and improved positioning accuracy. Once every bracket is positioned exactly where it needs to be, we transfer all the brackets onto your teeth simultaneously using a custom-made transfer tray.

Custom-Fabricated for Greater Precision

The custom fabrication process allows brackets to be positioned carefully before they are transferred to your teeth. This planned approach helps achieve greater consistency in bracket placement and supports more predictable orthodontic tooth movement.

By preparing the bracket positions outside the mouth, the orthodontist can work with better visibility and control, reducing the challenges associated with traditional direct bonding.

More Comfort, Less Chair Time

For patients, the difference can be noticeable. Indirect bonding simplifies the bracket placement appointment and makes the overall experience more comfortable.

With the brackets transferred together using a custom-made tray, appointments can be shorter, and patients spend less time keeping their mouths open during the procedure.

  • Shorter bracket placement appointments.
  • Less time spent keeping your mouth open.
  • Reduced jaw strain during the procedure.
  • A smoother and more comfortable treatment experience.
  • Greater control over bracket positioning.
More Predictable Tooth Movement

Accurate bracket positioning plays an important role in orthodontic treatment. When brackets are positioned carefully from the beginning, they help the orthodontist achieve better control over planned tooth movements.

This can contribute to more predictable treatment progress, fewer unexpected adjustments, and a more streamlined orthodontic journey.

Technology That Makes a Real Difference

At Sonnetsmile, we don't adopt technology simply because it sounds advanced. We choose techniques that genuinely improve patient comfort, clinical precision, and treatment outcomes.

Indirect bonding brings together careful planning, custom fabrication, and precise bracket transfer to deliver a better orthodontic experience without compromising on treatment quality.

Orthodontic Care Designed Around You

Orthodontic treatment shouldn't feel overwhelming. It should feel thoughtful, efficient, and personalized to your needs.

Discover a more precise and comfortable approach to braces with Sonnetsmile. Get in touch with our orthodontic team to learn how custom-fabricated braces and indirect bonding can make a difference in your smile journey.
