Why Sonnet?

14 Parameters. One smile. You.

A smile is more than just teeth. Inspired by the beauty of a 14-line sonnet, we believe every smile is a unique composition of carefully balanced details. From proportions, symmetry, and tooth display to gum contours, lips, and facial features, we study 14 essential parameters to understand what makes your smile unique. Founded by our beloved Ashwin D Rajan, Founder & Teeth Alignment Specialist, Sonnet is built on the belief that every smile deserves an individual approach. Our philosophy is never about creating a perfect copy, but about enhancing what makes you, you. Because the most beautiful smile isn't the most uniform one — it's the one that feels natural, reflects your personality, and brings out the best version of you.

Facing a Dental Emergency?

Dental emergencies can happen anytime. Whether you're experiencing a severe toothache, broken tooth, swelling, or any unexpected dental discomfort, Sonnet Smile is here to help. Get in touch with our team for prompt assistance and compassionate dental care in Chennai. Your smile deserves immediate attention!
