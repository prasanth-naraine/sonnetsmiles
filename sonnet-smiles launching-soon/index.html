Frequently Asking Questions

We don't just answer questions, we clear your doubts. Explore our frequently asked questions to find quick solutions. Still confused? We're here to make everything simple and clear. Your questions matter, and we're always happy to help!

1. Why Choose Sonnet Smile Lab?
At Sonnet Smile Lab, we bring out the best in every smile through gentle, advanced and personalised dental care. We combine modern technology, clinical precision and artistry to create natural-looking, confident smiles. Every treatment is thoughtfully planned around your individual dental needs and comfort.
2. How To Book Appointments?
You can contact Sonnet Smile Lab by Website Form, phone Call or WhatsApp to schedule your dental consultation. Our team will help you choose a convenient appointment time and guide you through the consultation process.
3. Are Appointments Necessary?
We recommend booking an appointment in advance to ensure adequate consultation time and a comfortable, personalised experience. Walk-in appointments may be accommodated depending on availability.
4. What Dental Treatments Are Available?
We offer personalised dental care covering preventive dentistry, restorative treatments, smile makeovers, teeth alignment, cosmetic dentistry, children's dentistry, dental implants and other general dental procedures. Treatment recommendations are based on your individual oral health requirements.
5. Do You Treat Children?
Yes. We understand that children may feel anxious about dental visits. Our child-friendly approach focuses on making every visit comfortable and reassuring. We also have the Consed Slim Master Nitrous Oxide Machine, which can help suitable young patients feel more relaxed during dental procedures.
6. What Is Nitrous Sedation?
Nitrous oxide sedation, commonly known as laughing gas sedation, is a method used to help patients feel relaxed during dental treatment. It is administered through a specially designed breathing mask and can help reduce dental anxiety. At Sonnet Smile Lab, we use the Consed Slim Master Nitrous Oxide Machine for suitable patients following clinical assessment.
7. What Dental Technology Do You Use?
We integrate advanced dental technology to support accurate diagnosis, comfortable treatments and personalised smile planning. Our technology includes digital intraoral scanning, CBCT imaging, dental laser technology, facial scanning, precision aligner finishing and modern sterilisation equipment.
8. What Is CBCT Imaging?
Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) is an advanced dental imaging technique that provides detailed three-dimensional views of the teeth, jawbones and surrounding structures. It helps dentists assess complex dental conditions and plan procedures such as dental implant placement with greater precision when clinically indicated.
9. What Is Digital Scanning?
A digital intraoral scanner captures detailed digital images of your teeth and oral structures without requiring traditional impression materials in many situations. It makes the impression-taking process more convenient and comfortable while helping plan dental restorations, orthodontic aligners and smile transformations.
10. Do You Offer Clear Aligners?
Yes. Clear aligner treatment may be suitable for correcting selected teeth alignment and bite concerns. Digital scanning and precision aligner finishing technology help support the planning and fabrication process, contributing to a comfortable and personalised orthodontic experience.
11. What Is Digital Smile Design?
Digital smile design is a technology-assisted approach to planning smile improvements. It considers your teeth, facial proportions, smile characteristics and individual preferences to help visualise and plan a smile that complements your natural facial appearance.
12. Why Is Facial Scanning Important?
Facial scanning captures your facial proportions and features, helping the dentist understand how your teeth and smile relate to your overall facial appearance. At Sonnet Smile Lab, RayFace facial scanning technology supports personalised smile assessment and treatment planning.
13. How Do You Ensure Sterilisation?
Patient safety and infection control are among our highest priorities. We use the W&H Lina Autoclave, which employs validated steam sterilisation processes to sterilise reusable dental instruments. We follow appropriate hygiene and sterilisation protocols to maintain a safe clinical environment.
14. Are Dental Lasers Painful?
Dental laser technology can be used for selected dental and gum procedures with precision and minimal disturbance to surrounding tissues. Depending on the procedure and individual condition, laser treatment may offer benefits such as reduced bleeding and improved comfort. Suitability is determined following a dental examination.
